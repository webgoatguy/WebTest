How to play
  • Blue starts owning all 19 points along the left edge; Red starts owning all 19 points along the right edge. Tap one of your own nodes, then tap a nearby point (within 3 king-moves) to grow a new edge there. Tap your selected node again to cancel it — any other tap attempts a move from it.
  • Landing on an empty point claims it. Landing on your own point closes a loop. Landing on an opponent's point (any of theirs, including their home-edge nodes) attacks it: every edge attached to that node gets cut, the node itself becomes yours, and it's illegal to attempt if that node has too many edges for the current phase's cap (below).
  • Your edge's path can't pass through one of your own existing nodes, and a brand-new node of yours can't land on one of your own existing edges. You also can't lay a new edge on top of an existing one, yours or theirs. None of this applies to your opponent's nodes or edges — your edge can freely pass through or near their stuff.
  • The game runs for exactly 200 "decisions" (one decision = one edge+node placement), split into two 100-decision phases. In Phase 1 you can cut at most 1 opponent edge per move — from crossing, from an attack, or both combined. In Phase 2, up to 2.
  • Cutting a key edge deletes everything downstream that's no longer connected to that player's home edge.
  • Each node is worth points based on how far it sits from your own home edge — 0 right at home, rising by 1 for each column deeper into enemy territory. Whoever has more total points when the 200 decisions run out wins (a tie is possible).
